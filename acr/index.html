Progress statement

This report covers 29 years of continuous development. The Marine Corps installed the discipline. Eight years as a professor and department chair added teaching, budgets and the patience to run a committee. Tri-C is where accessibility became the job, with a million course materials and four campuses to bring along.

Since 2021 the work has run from the vendor side. At Accessiblü I've embedded with product and engineering teams, written the VPATs, briefed executives and city councils, and in July 2026 took over the business. Around that work I've built tooling with AI, so audits that used to take days of spreadsheet work now start from an inventory and a guided checklist.

The open items in this report are isolated to one area, experience as an employee inside a large matrixed organization, and each carries a documented plan in the roadmap.

Roadmap

ACRs list known gaps with a plan. Here's mine, mapped to the role's first year.

  • First 30 days

    Learn the matrix

    Meet the owners of Primer, the Fundamentals scorecards and the accessibility champions network. Map how an accessibility issue moves from report to fix today.

  • First 90 days

    Open the listening channels

    Stand up a regular feedback loop with developers with disabilities and the Enterprise Accessibility Advisory Panel, and publish what we heard and what changed.

  • First year

    Grow the scanner

    Ship scanner plugins for checks axe-core can't make from a snapshot, starting with focus visibility, and help Enterprise customers wire the scanner into their pipelines.
